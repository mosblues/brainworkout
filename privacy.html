Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Brain Workout Books handles information submitted through the email signup form on our website.

Information we collect

When you join the Brain Workout VIP Club, we collect the email address you provide. We also record the date and time of your signup and the signup form's source.

How we use your information

We use your email address to manage your signup, provide access to the free book or related benefits, and communicate about Brain Workout Books, including updates, new releases, and offers related to the VIP Club.

Where information is stored

Signup information is stored using Google services, including Google Sheets and Google Apps Script. These services process information according to their applicable privacy terms.

Spam protection: Cloudflare Turnstile

We use Cloudflare Turnstile to protect our signup form against automated submissions and abuse. Turnstile may process technical information about your browser and interaction with the website to assess whether a submission is legitimate. This verification may operate invisibly.

For more information about how Cloudflare processes information through Turnstile, please read the Cloudflare Turnstile Privacy Addendum and Cloudflare Privacy Policy.

Data retention and requests

We retain signup information as needed to manage the VIP Club and related communications. You can contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your email information, subject to applicable law.

Contact

For privacy-related questions or requests, email info@brainworkoutbooks.com.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes in our website or practices. The date at the top of this page indicates when it was last updated.
